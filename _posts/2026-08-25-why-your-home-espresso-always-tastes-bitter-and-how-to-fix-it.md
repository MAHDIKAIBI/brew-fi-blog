If your home espresso machine consistently pulls harsh, bitter shots despite using expensive equipment, the primary culprit is hidden late-stage extraction chemistry. You will learn how fines and boulders cause localized channeling, how oxidized surface lipids corrupt fresh espresso, and how specific brew variables like water temperature and extraction time alter solubles. Discover actionable, step-by-step dialing techniques using the single-variable rule to eliminate harshness and consistently pull sweet, balanced espresso at home.
