Viewers will learn how particle size distribution dictates water velocity, why measuring dose and yield strictly by mass eliminates visual volume errors, and how needle distribution tools prevent channeling under nine bars of pressure. Furthermore, this guide examines the precise chemistry of coffee bean degassing and explains how water mineral content affects extraction yield and long-term machine maintenance.
